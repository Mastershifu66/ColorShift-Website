ColorShift
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

ColorShift is a browser extension that allows users to customize the visual appearance of webpages.

Data Collection

ColorShift does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information.

ColorShift does not collect names, email addresses, passwords, financial information, location information, browsing history, or personal communications.

The extension processes webpage styling locally in the user's browser solely to provide its color customization functionality.

Data Sharing

ColorShift does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

External Services

ColorShift does not send webpage content or user information to external servers.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
